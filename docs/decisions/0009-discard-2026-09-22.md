# 0009. 22/09 is discarded

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Status:** accepted

## Context

`nivel2_trades_20260922.csv` only has a header, no trades. Its `nivel3_operaciones_20260922.csv` does contain 30 operations, produced by an old version of the script: they have no per-leg detail, and the single-leg structure does not say whether they are calls or puts.

## Decision

22/09 is discarded, as the specification says. The data report shows it as "level 3 without level 2".

## Rejected alternatives

- **Importing the 30 level-3 operations in a degraded mode**: they could not be classified reliably (each leg's side is missing and, for single-legs, the type too), and it would open a second loading path for a single day.

## Consequences

Those 30 operations do not appear in the dashboard. If they were wanted later, this decision would be revisited.
